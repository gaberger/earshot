## Fixed

- **The offer to record a call now shows up.** Earshot noticed Zoom, Teams, Meet and other calls, but the
  offer never reached you: macOS does not let this build of Earshot show notifications, and the fallback
  notice sat inside Earshot's window, behind the call. The offer is now a small panel at the top right of
  the screen: **Zoom is using the microphone. Record this meeting?** with **Record** and **Not for Zoom**.
  It floats over full-screen calls, does not take the keyboard from the call, and goes away by itself
  when you start recording, when the call ends, or after two minutes. It still only asks; it never
  records without your click.
- **A recording that stops by itself no longer leaves the button saying Stop.** When a capture ended on
  its own, for example because the disk filled up, Earshot kept the audio and transcribed it, but the
  Record button and the menu bar timer carried on as if it were still recording. They now reset, and
  Earshot tells you why the recording stopped. A full disk gets its own message telling you to free space.

Everything else is unchanged, so updating changes nothing you already use.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.2.1.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**. After an update, macOS may also forget the Accessibility permission:
if Dictation stops typing, switch Earshot off and on in **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.2.1`.
