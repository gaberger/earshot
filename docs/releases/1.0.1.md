## Fixed

- **Summaries of long recordings no longer fail.** A long meeting could stop with
  "Summary failed" because its transcript was too big for Apple's on-device model. Earshot now
  measures transcripts with the model's own token counts. It splits a long one into parts, takes
  notes on each part, and condenses the notes until they fit, so even a recording hours long
  gets a summary.
- **Recordings in languages written without spaces** (such as Japanese and Chinese) are now split
  correctly instead of being sent to the model in one piece.
- If a recording truly can't be summarized on this Mac, Earshot now says so plainly instead of
  showing a model error.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.0.1.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.0.1`.
