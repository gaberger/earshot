## New

- **Earshot stops recording by itself when the meeting is over.** If no one speaks for 5 minutes,
  the recording stops, then it is saved, transcribed and summarized as usual. For the last 30
  seconds you get a beep and a countdown, and **Keep recording** carries on. Any speech cancels the
  countdown.
- **Choose how long it waits** under **Settings → Recording → Stop when quiet**: 3, 5, 10, 15 or
  30 minutes, or Never.
- It is careful not to cut a meeting short. It judges speech against how loud the room is, so a
  noisy room and a quiet one both work. It does not stop before it has heard some speech and the
  recording is at least 2 minutes long. A Mac that sleeps, or a microphone that drops out, is not
  counted as silence. A recording where no one ever speaks stops after 15 minutes.

Earshot hears the room through the microphone, so a call you listen to on headphones sounds
one-sided. For those, choose a longer time or Never.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.0.2.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.0.2`.
