## New

- **Send with your voice: say "exclamation point".** A new option, off until you switch it on, lets you end a
  dictation with the words **exclamation point** (or **exclamation mark**). Earshot types the rest without them
  and then presses **Control-Return**, for tools that take Control-Return to send, such as waking an agent in
  Claude Code. Say only those words and Earshot types nothing and just presses Control-Return.
  - Turn it on under **Settings → Dictation → Submit → Say "exclamation point" to send Control-Return**.
  - Only the spoken words at the very end count. A "!" that the speech model adds to an excited sentence does
    nothing, and the words in the middle of a sentence are typed as you said them.
  - It works with **Tidy up what I say**: the command is taken from what you said before the tidy-up runs.
  - It sends the key only when your words were really typed where you started. If you switch windows, if
    Accessibility is not allowed, or if a secure field is active, nothing is sent, and Earshot tells you when
    you used only the command.
  - While it is on, the words "exclamation point" or "exclamation mark" at the very end of a dictation are
    not typed. It sends Control-Return instead of plain Return, never both.

Also new in the project, outside the app: an **Acknowledgements** page that credits the projects that inspired
Earshot and lists the components it uses with their licenses, and a published website at
gaberger.github.io/earshot. The in-app Help and the user guide describe the new option.

Everything else is unchanged, so updating changes nothing you already use. The new option is off by default.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.2.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**. After an update, macOS may also forget the Accessibility permission:
if Dictation stops typing, switch Earshot off and on in **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.2.0`.
