## New

- **Dictate into any app.** Turn on **Settings → Dictation → Dictate into any app**, click into any
  text field (a web form, an email, a chat), hold **Control + Option + Space** (or a shortcut you choose), speak, and let go.
  Earshot types what you said where your cursor is. It transcribes on your Mac with the same Whisper
  model as your recordings, and the audio is deleted straight away.
  - A small **Listening…** indicator with a live level bar shows while it hears you, then
    **Transcribing…** until the text arrives. It never takes focus from your field.
  - **Change the shortcut** under Settings → Dictation → Shortcut. If another app already uses it,
    Earshot tells you.
  - The microphone is only on while you hold the keys.
  - It never types into password fields, and it stays off while a meeting is being recorded.
  - It types by pasting, then puts back whatever was on your clipboard.
  - It needs **Microphone** and **Accessibility** access; Earshot asks when you turn it on.

Dictation is off until you turn it on, so updating changes nothing you already use.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.1.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**. After an update, macOS may also forget the Accessibility permission:
if Dictation stops typing, switch Earshot off and on in **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.1.0`.
