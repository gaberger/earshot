## New

- **Earshot offers to record when a call starts.** When Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime or any other app
  has been using the microphone for about 10 seconds, a notification asks **Record this meeting?**
  Click **Record** to start. It only asks; it never records without your click. It asks once per
  call. Choose **Not for this app** to stop it asking about that app.
- **Open Earshot at login.** Earshot has to be running to notice a call, so turn this on under
  **Settings → Recording** and it waits in the menu bar. You can turn the offer off in the same
  place with **Offer to record when a call starts**.

macOS asks for permission to show notifications the first time Earshot has an offer. If you say no,
Earshot shows the offer inside its window instead.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.0.4.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.0.4`.
