## Fixed

- **Earshot now stops after a call, as 1.0.2 should have.** 1.0.2 judged the end of a meeting only
  by loudness, and ordinary room noise kept resetting its timer, so it often never stopped.

## New

- **Earshot notices when you are on a call.** While Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime or any other app is
  using the microphone, Earshot keeps recording, however quiet the room is. About a minute after
  that app lets go of the microphone, Earshot stops: a 30-second countdown with a beep, and
  **Keep recording** carries on. Joining another call cancels the countdown.
- **Calls you hear on headphones now work.** Earshot no longer depends on hearing the call through
  the room.
- Meetings with no call app (a conversation in the room) still stop after the quiet time you chose
  under **Settings → Recording → Stop when quiet**.

If another app keeps the microphone open (a dictation tool, a browser tab left on a meeting page),
Earshot keeps recording until you press Stop.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.0.3.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.0.3`.
